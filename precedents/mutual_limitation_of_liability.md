# Precedent: Balanced Mutual Limitation of Liability

**Risk Category:** FINANCIAL_EXPOSURE
**Market Standard:** Mutual aggregate liability cap equal to 12 months fees paid or payable; mutual waiver of indirect, consequential, and punitive damages.

## Recommended Standard Language
"To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law: (a) neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or loss of profits or revenue; and (b) each party's total cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid or payable by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability."

## Market Rationale
Zero-dollar ($0) liability disclaimers for vendors combined with uncapped customer liability create unacceptable business risk asymmetry. A mutual 12-month trailing fee cap is standard in commercial SaaS and enterprise services.
