# Precedent: Commercial Lease Capital Improvements Amortization & Landlord Entry

**Risk Category:** FINANCIAL_EXPOSURE / ASYMMETRIC_OBLIGATIONS
**Market Standard:** Capital improvements amortized over useful life; landlord access conditioned upon 24-hour advance written notice.

## Recommended Standard Language
"Operating expenses shall exclude any capital replacements or structural modernization, except where mandated by law or directly resulting in operating cost savings. Any permissible capital improvements shall be amortized on a straight-line basis over their reasonable useful life in accordance with GAAP, and Tenant shall pay only its proportionate share of the annual amortized amount during the lease term. Landlord may enter the Premises during business hours only upon at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, except in cases of bona fide emergency threatening immediate property damage."

## Market Rationale
Uncapped pass-through of building-wide structural improvements at landlord sole discretion creates open-ended financial exposure. Unannounced entry without notice infringes tenant privacy and commercial confidentiality.
