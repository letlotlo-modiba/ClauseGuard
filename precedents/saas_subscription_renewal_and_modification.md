# Precedent: SaaS Renewal Notice and Bilateral Modification

**Risk Category:** UNILATERAL_MODIFICATION / FINANCIAL_EXPOSURE
**Market Standard:** 30-60 day written notice of price increases with penalty-free opt-out; mutual written agreement for material term amendments.

## Recommended Standard Language
"Provider may modify subscription fees upon renewal provided Provider delivers written notice of the proposed adjustment at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the current Subscription Term. If Subscriber does not agree to the adjusted fees, Subscriber may terminate the subscription effective at the end of the current term without penalty. Any material amendments to these Terms of Service shall require mutual written agreement or thirty (30) days advance notice with the right for Subscriber to cancel and receive a pro-rata refund of unearned prepaid fees."

## Market Rationale
Allowing unilateral modification of pricing and SLA terms with "continued use deemed acceptance" denies customers the fundamental benefit of their bargain.
