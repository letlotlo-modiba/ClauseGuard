# Precedent: Market-Standard Mutual Indemnification

**Risk Category:** FINANCIAL_EXPOSURE
**Market Standard:** Mutual, fault-based, and capped to aggregate fees paid in preceding 12 months.

## Recommended Standard Language
"Each party ("Indemnifying Party") shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party, its officers, directors, and employees from and against any third-party claims, suits, liabilities, damages, and reasonable legal expenses to the extent arising directly from: (a) the Indemnifying Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct; (b) material breach of this Agreement; or (c) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by deliverables provided hereunder. Neither party shall be liable for indemnification exceeding the aggregate fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim."

## Market Rationale
One-way uncapped indemnification exposes service providers or smaller counterparties to potentially fatal litigation defense costs. Market standard agreements require bilateral indemnification with clear causation and financial caps tied to the economic value of the contract.
