# Precedent: Standard Bilateral NDA with Reasonable Duration and Exceptions

**Risk Category:** ASYMMETRIC_OBLIGATIONS / IP_AND_RIGHTS
**Market Standard:** 2-3 year confidentiality duration; standard exceptions proven under ordinary civil standards; mutual prevailing-party fee recovery.

## Recommended Standard Language
"Confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall expire two (2) years following the date of disclosure. Information shall not be considered Confidential Information if it: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of Receiving Party; (b) was already known to Receiving Party without restriction; or (c) is independently developed without reference to Disclosing Party's information. In any action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney fees and costs."

## Market Rationale
Perpetual confidentiality on exploratory talks is commercially impractical. Heightened standards of proof ("clear and convincing documentary evidence") shift litigation risk unfairly, and one-way legal fee shifting encourages frivolous injunctions.
