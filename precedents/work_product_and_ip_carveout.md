# Precedent: Work Product IP Assignment with Pre-Existing IP Carve-Out

**Risk Category:** IP_AND_RIGHTS
**Market Standard:** Assignment restricted to custom deliverables created specifically for client; explicit retention and non-exclusive license for pre-existing tools and background IP.

## Recommended Standard Language
"Upon receipt of full payment, Contractor assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to custom deliverables developed specifically for Company under the applicable Statement of Work. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Contractor retains sole ownership of all pre-existing tools, libraries, methodologies, and background IP owned or developed by Contractor prior to or independently of this Agreement ("Contractor Pre-Existing IP"). Contractor grants Company a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Contractor Pre-Existing IP solely as embedded within the custom deliverables."

## Market Rationale
Demanding assignment of all prior works, unrelated inventions, and worldwide moral rights destroys a contractor or agency's ability to reuse their own codebase, libraries, or tooling with other clients.
