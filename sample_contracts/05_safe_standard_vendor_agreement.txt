STANDARD PROCUREMENT AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made between GreenValley Logistics ("Customer") and Apex Office Supplies ("Supplier").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Supplier shall provide procurement, office supplies fulfillment, and scheduled delivery services as described in mutually signed purchase orders.

2. PRICING AND PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoice amounts within thirty (30) days of receipt. All rates are fixed for the initial term.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for twelve (12) months. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon thirty (30) days advance written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other party breaches a material term and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of written notice.

4. MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall safeguard the proprietary business information of the other party using the same degree of care it exercises for its own confidential data. Confidentiality obligations shall endure for two (2) years following expiration.

5. MUTUAL LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party shall be liable to the other for special, incidental, or consequential damages. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be capped at the total amount paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

6. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION
Each party shall defend and indemnify the other party against third-party claims resulting directly from the indemnifying party's gross negligence, intentional misconduct, or violation of applicable laws.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be construed under the laws of the State of Illinois. The parties agree to submit to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois.
