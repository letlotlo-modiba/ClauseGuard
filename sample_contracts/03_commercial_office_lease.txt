COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between Metro Commercial Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Nexus Design Studio ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant Suite 400 located at 1200 Market Street, consisting of approximately 3,200 rentable square feet, for general commercial office use.

2. LEASE TERM
The term of this Lease shall be thirty-six (36) months commencing on January 1, 2027, and ending on December 31, 2029.

3. BASE RENT
Tenant agrees to pay monthly base rent of $8,500.00 in advance on the first day of each calendar month. Late payments shall incur a five percent (5%) late penalty.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $17,000.00 as security for full performance of Tenant's obligations.

5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND CAPITAL PASS-THROUGHS
Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of building operating expenses. In addition, Landlord may at its sole discretion pass through to Tenant any capital improvements, structural replacements, and building modernization costs incurred by Landlord during the term without limitation or amortized cap.

6. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
Landlord will provide standard water, HVAC during regular business hours (8am-6pm M-F), and common area cleaning. Tenant pays for electricity and internet.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall at its own expense keep the interior premises in good order and condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

8. EARLY TERMINATION PENALTY
If Tenant terminates this Lease prior to the expiration of the full 36-month term for any reason, Tenant shall immediately pay Landlord an early termination penalty equal to the entire remaining balance of the unexpired lease term as accelerated liquidated damages.

9. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY
Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against any liability occurring on the premises.

10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Tenant may not assign or sublet the premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold in its sole and absolute discretion.

11. LANDLORD ACCESS AND ENTRY
Landlord and Landlord's agents reserve the right to enter the premises at any hour, with or without prior notice to Tenant, for inspection, repairs, or showing to prospective buyers.
